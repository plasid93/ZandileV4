NANNYBEAR

Version: Cloud-synced household employment manager

New features:
- Detailed employee profile and employment details
- 3-month probation tracker and reviews
- Attendance calendar with arrival/departure times
- Advanced annual, sick, family responsibility and other leave management
- Leave requests with pending/approved/declined/cancelled status
- Working-day leave calculations (Monday-Friday)
- Expanded time-in-lieu bank with categories and conversion of 8 hours to 1 leave day
- Saturday, Sunday and public-holiday work tracking
- South African public holidays
- Salary due date and payment history
- Downloadable PDF payslips
- Performance and notes log
- Access / household notes
- Emergency and important information fields
- Optional UIF administration tracker
- Birthdays and important dates
- Local JSON backup and restore

SYNC NOTE
This version uses Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database to share the household data securely between approved users. Google sign-in is remembered on the device, and the app shows whether changes are offline, saving, or synced.
